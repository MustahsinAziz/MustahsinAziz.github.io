About Me

Hello! I’m Mustahsin‑Ul Aziz, an Environmental Economist and development practitioner currently pursuing a Ph.D. at West Virginia University. My work focuses on practical solutions to complex socio‑environmental challenges using data science, remote sensing, and policy analysis.

I’ve led randomized trials, contributed to multi‑hundred‑million‑dollar projects, and now study how environmental quality shapes labor markets and public health.

Outside research, you’ll find me cooking biryani, making music, and exploring trails.
